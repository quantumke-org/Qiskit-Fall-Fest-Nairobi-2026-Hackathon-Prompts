# 🤖 AI Usage Policy

AI tools are **allowed and encouraged** during the Qiskit Fall Fest Nairobi 2026 Hackathon.

The hackathon is designed to help participants learn quantum computing, and AI can be a valuable learning and development tool.

You may use tools such as:

* ChatGPT
* Claude
* Gemini
* GitHub Copilot
* Other AI assistants

---

## ✅ What You Can Use AI For

Participants may use AI to:

* Learn quantum computing concepts
* Understand Qiskit code and documentation
* Generate code
* Debug errors
* Get hints and explanations
* Explore different approaches
* Improve code
* Brainstorm ideas
* Develop stretch challenges
* Document their solution

---

## ⚠️ Your Responsibility

You are responsible for your final submission.

You should be able to:

* Understand what your code does
* Explain the quantum concepts used
* Test your implementation
* Identify and fix errors
* Verify that your solution meets the challenge requirements

> **Do not blindly trust AI-generated answers or code.**

AI tools can generate incorrect explanations, outdated APIs or code that does not work.

Always **run, test and verify** your solution.

---

## 💡 Recommended Use of AI

We encourage participants to use AI as a **learning partner**, not simply as a solution generator.

Instead of:

> "Give me the complete solution."

Try:

> "I'm new to quantum computing. Explain the concept I need to solve this challenge without giving me the solution."

Or:

> "Here is my Qiskit code. Help me understand why it isn't producing the expected result."

Or:

> "Give me a hint about how I can approach this problem without giving me the answer."

---

## 📝 AI Disclosure

If AI played a significant role in your solution, briefly disclose how you used it in your submission.

For example:

```text
AI Tool: ChatGPT

How I used it:
I used ChatGPT to understand quantum superposition,
debug my Qiskit circuit and explore different approaches.
```

AI disclosure is **not intended to penalize participants**.

---

## 🚫 What Is Not Allowed

Participants must not:

* Submit another participant's work as their own
* Copy another participant's complete solution
* Misrepresent another person's work as their own
* Use AI to impersonate another participant or manipulate judging

Using AI-generated code is permitted, provided that the participant understands, tests and takes responsibility for the submitted work.

---

## 🔍 Verification

Organizers may ask a participant to provide clarification about their submission if there are concerns about originality or understanding.

Participants should therefore keep their:

* Notebook
* Code
* Experiment results
* Relevant AI interactions/prompts

where possible.

---

# 🎯 Our Philosophy

This hackathon is **not a test of who can code without AI**.

It is an opportunity to learn how modern tools can help you explore and solve problems in quantum computing.

We encourage you to:

**Prompt → Learn → Experiment → Question → Build → Test → Improve**

Use AI.
Question it.
Verify it.
Learn from it.

Most importantly, **understand what you submit.** ⚛️
